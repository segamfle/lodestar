Lodestar

On-chain wagering games, built against the Chain casino SDK.

  • Tideline

    Six rungs stand above the waterline. Spread your wager across the ladder, then watch the tide come in — every rung the water reaches pays out. 96% return, identical on every rung and every spread.

  • Constellation

    Twenty-five cells of dark sky. Mark a shape of three to six cells, then seven stars light at random — you are paid on how much of your shape came alight. 96% return, identical whichever shape you draw.

Source at github.com/segamfle/lodestar. Built for Chain Jam Vol. 1.
